Why add the correction?

Compression can leave useful measurements and trends insufficiently expressed. TRB sees current values, missingness, running / recent history, changes, rates and observation age. It learns nonlinear corrections without replacing R-GCN, MRTF or the TCT. XGBoost is established; the contribution is this temporal integration.

How the methodology changes

Stage 1: fit the shared neural model with validation early stopping. Stage 2: freeze it and fit correction heads on training patients. Validation selects settings and correction strengths; test patients only score the final pair. Neural seed 73 and split seed 42 are frozen defaults. No future outcome is a prediction input.

How to interpret the evidence

Current scores and gains are generated in docs/MEASURED_RESULTS.md and its JSON snapshot. Read all metrics and bootstrap intervals, including unfavorable changes. The authors' original clinical cohort is unavailable. Local gains do not prove superiority on that cohort, universal improvement, identified treatment effects or clinical validity.

Source: Sharma & Kaur (2025), IEEE Access 13, 150010-150027. DOI · Supplied paper.
